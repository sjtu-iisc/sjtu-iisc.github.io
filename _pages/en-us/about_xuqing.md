**Qing Xu**

**Post-doctoral Fellow**

**Affiliation**: School of Aeronautics and Astronautics

**Research interests**: Multi-source Information Fusion, Complex Systems Engineering Management, Data-driven System Optimization and Control.

**Brief introduction**: 
Research on modeling, sensing, and optimal control of complex dynamic systems. To achieve state reconstruction and fault diagnosis of systems based on multi-source heterogeneous data, enabling perception and prediction of complex system operational states. To identify key state variables and their coupling relationships to achieve autonomous resource allocation and strategy optimization. To construct data-driven decision support systems, conduct causal inference modeling and multi-source indicator fusion, and provide management decision support for engineering scenarios.
