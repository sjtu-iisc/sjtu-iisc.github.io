Brief Introduction

The Institute of Intelligent Sensing and Control at Shanghai Jiao Tong University is dedicated to addressing major national strategic needs and advancing the frontiers of science and technology. Focusing on the deep integration of artificial intelligence with intelligent aerospace systems, the Institute has long been engaged in fundamental research and key technological innovation in areas such as “AI+X” algorithms, multi-agent game and collaboration, and the innovative evolution of autonomous systems. Guided by original scientific challenges, the Institute pursues academic breakthroughs; through major national missions, it strengthens and refines its technological体系; and through real-world engineering applications, it drives the closed-loop enhancement of capabilities. It is committed to building a high-level innovation platform that combines theoretical depth, system-level competence, and strong engineering translation capabilities. Driven by its self-developed “Lv Shang” multimodal foundation model, the Institute has established a coordinated technological framework centered on models, algorithms, data, and infrastructure. This framework encompasses large models for aerospace systems, game-theoretic large models, multimodal large models, intelligent algorithms for autonomous guidance and control, domain-specific data pools, and high-fidelity simulation and training platforms. Based on this foundation, the Institute has formed a complete innovation chain spanning foundational model development, core algorithm research, system validation, and scenario deployment, continuously enhancing the intelligent perception and control capabilities of aerospace systems. With innovative basic research at its core, the Institute has undertaken more than 30 major projects funded by the National Natural Science Foundation of China, the Science and Technology Commission of the Central Military Commission, the Equipment Development Department, the State Administration of Science, Technology and Industry for National Defense, the High-Resolution Earth Observation Program, the National High-Tech R&D Program (863 Program), and major initiatives in the BeiDou Navigation Satellite System and deep-space exploration. Through mechanism innovation and technological research, the Institute has achieved over RMB 100 million both in research funding and in capacity building, and has established a relatively complete research ecosystem with strong scientific platforms, experimental facilities, computing resources, and engineering validation capabilities. In response to emerging opportunities in commercial aerospace and the development of integrated aerospace intelligent systems, the Institute has strategically advanced coordinated development across both the space and air domains. In the space domain, it focuses on intelligent aerospace perception, autonomous satellite guidance and control, constellation collaborative planning, space-based multimodal perception, and orbital game theory. In the air domain, it focuses on the low-altitude economy and intelligent aviation applications, with sustained efforts in areas such as heterogeneous UAV swarm cooperative transport and autonomous maritime replenishment. Through these efforts, the Institute is promoting the deep integration of artificial intelligence into the entire value chain of commercial aerospace and low-altitude intelligent equipment, including R&D, manufacturing, operation, and services, while accelerating the development of an interdisciplinary innovation ecosystem for integrated aerospace intelligent systems. The Institute firmly believes that first-class platforms attract first-class talent, and is committed to building a well-structured, internationally competitive faculty and research team with a clear talent pipeline. It currently has 5 senior-title faculty members, 10 mid-level-title faculty members, and 3 nationally recognized experts, and has brought together a number of outstanding scholars from leading universities ranked among the world’s top 20. This has fostered a coordinated talent structure featuring leading experts, young core researchers, and a strong reserve of emerging talent. Members of the team have been selected for prestigious programs and honors such as the Shanghai Outstanding Academic Leaders Program, the Shanghai Magnolia Talent Program, and the Shanghai Super Postdoctoral Incentive Program. They have also received distinctions including one of Shanghai Jiao Tong University’s Top Ten Science and Technology Advancement Awards, recognition as experts for major national on-orbit service projects, and the title of Shanghai Top Ten Outstanding Young Persons. The Institute’s achievements and team stories have received sustained media attention from CCTV, People’s Daily, The Paper, Sina News, the official website of the Government of the Macao Special Administrative Region, Macao Radio and Television, and the front-page headlines of Shanghai Jiao Tong University. The Institute places particular emphasis on the central role of young talent and has established an integrated talent development system linking tenure-track faculty, full-time research staff, and postdoctoral researchers. It provides strong support for outstanding young scholars in areas such as research platforms, graduate student quotas, team resource allocation, and applications for talent programs. Leveraging Shanghai Jiao Tong University’s first-class disciplinary strengths and forward-looking development strategy in integrated aerospace systems, the Institute warmly invites outstanding young scholars and high-level talent with international vision, originality, interdisciplinary backgrounds, and a passion for engineering to join us.
